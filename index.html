ABOUT

Hi, I’m Aila.

I’m a yoga teacher who loves creating classes that feel welcoming, grounded, and intentional. My teaching blends accessible breath-to-movement flows with time to slow down, feel, and connect.

I also weave sound into my classes, especially during savasana, to create a softer landing at the end of practice.
